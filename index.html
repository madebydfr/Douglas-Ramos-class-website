My name is Douglas Ramos,
I'm a Designer, videographer, business owner and content creator.

I am a 26-year-old freelance graphic designer and multidisciplinary creative based in Los Angeles, originally from El Salvador.
With an Associate Degree in Studio Arts from East Los Angeles College, further education at Otis College of Art and Design, and ongoing Design studies at California State University, Dominguez Hills, I have developed a strong foundation in visual storytelling, composition, and concept development.
As the founder and creative director of Die2BReborn, I oversee art direction, graphic design, typography, photography, videography, marketing, content creation, and analytics.
Rooted in identity, transformation, faith, and personal experience, my work turns meaningful ideas into cohesive visual stories that leave a lasting impression.
